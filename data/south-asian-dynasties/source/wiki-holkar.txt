L151: The Holkars (pronunciation: 【69†[ɦo(ː)ɭkəɾ]】) were the ruling house of the 【70†Indore State】 of the 【71†Maratha Confederacy】, and earlier held the rank of 【72†Subahdar】 under Peshwa 【73†Baji Rao I】 of the 【74†Maratha Empire】. When the Maratha Confederacy began to weaken due to internal clashes, the Holkars declared themselves the rulers of 【70†Indore】 in Central 【75†India】, existing as an autonomous member of the 【71†Maratha Confederacy】 until 1818.
L152: Later, their kingdom became a 【76†princely state】 under the British protection.
L153: 【77†Usha Devi Holkar】, 15th Maharani of Indore, is the current titular head.[ 1 ]
L154: ## Rise of Holkar as Subahdar
L155: 
L156: 【322†Image†en.wikipedia.org】Fort Ahilya in 【78†Maheshwar】 【323†Image†en.wikipedia.org】A Statue of 【79†Ahilyabai Holkar】 at Datta Temple【324†Image†en.wikipedia.org】The gate of 【67†Rajwada】, royal palace of the Holkar dynasty, 【68†Indore】
L157: 
L158: The Holkars were of 【80†Dhangar】 【81†Maratha】 origin.[ 2 ][ 3 ][ 4 ][ 5 ]
L159: 【65†Malhar Rao Holkar】 (1694–1766), was born in Murum in 【82†Satara district】 in a 【80†Dhangar】 family and was a Maratha chief serving under 【83†Bajirao I】, was given the area of 【70†Indore】 to look after by Bajirao 1,the Peshwa of 【84†Shahu I】. In the 1720s, he led 【85†Maratha armies】 in the 【86†Malwa】 region under the Peshwa Bajirao, and in 1733 was granted 9 【87†parganas】 in the vicinity of 【68†Indore】 by the Peshwas.
L160: The township of Indore already existed as an independent principality established by 【88†Nandlal Mandloi】 of 【89†Kampel】. 【88†Nandlal Mandloi】 was won over by the Maratha forces and he allowed them to camp across the Khan River. In 1734, Malhar Rao established a camp later called Malharganj. In 1747, he began construction of his royal palace, the 【67†Rajwada】.
L161: By the time of his death, he looked after much of Malwa for the Maratha Empire controlled by their lords Peshwas, and was acknowledged as one of the five houses of the 【74†Maratha Empire】.[【90†citation needed】]
L162: He was succeeded by 【79†Ahilyabai Holkar】 (r. 1767–1795), his daughter-in-law. She was born in the Chaundi village in 【91†Maharashtra】. She moved the capital to 【78†Maheshwar】, south of Indore on the 【92†Narmada River】. Rani Ahilyabai was a prolific builder and patron of 【93†Hindu】 temples in Maheshwar and Indore. She also built temples at sacred sites outside her kingdom, from 【94†Dwarka】 in 【95†Gujarat】 east to the 【96†Kashi Vishwanath Temple】 at 【97†Varanasi】 on the 【98†Ganges】.[【90†citation needed】]
L163: The adopted son of Malhar Rao Holkar, 【99†Tukoji Rao Holkar】 (r. 1795–1797) briefly succeeded Rani Ahilyabai upon her death. Tukoji Rao had been a commander under Ahilyabai for her entire rule.[【90†citation needed】]
L164: ## The fall of the Maratha Empire
L165: 
L166: 【325†Image†en.wikipedia.org】【100†Yashwantrao Holkar】.
L167: 
L168: His son 【100†Yashwantrao Holkar】 (r. 1797–1811) (also called as Jaswant Rao) succeeded Tukoji Rao Holkar upon his death. He tried to free the Delhi 【101†Mughal Emperor】 【102†Shah Alam II】 from the British in the unsuccessful 【103†Second Anglo-Maratha War】. The grateful Shah Alam gave him the title of Maharajadiraj Rajrajeshwar Alija Bahadur in honor of his bravery.[【90†citation needed】]
L169: 【326†Image†en.wikipedia.org】Tukojirao Holkar II, Indore, from a drawing by Mr. W. Carpenter, Jun.," from the Illustrated London News, 1857
L170: 
L171: Attempts by Yashwantrao Holkar to unite the kings failed, and he was approached to sign a peace treaty with the British. The Treaty of Rajghat, signed late December 1805, recognised him as a sovereign king.[【90†citation needed】]
L172: ## Battle of Mahidpur
L173: 
L174: 【327†Image†en.wikipedia.org】【104†Tukojirao Holkar III】 Maharaja Holkar of Indore
L175: In 1811, the four-year-old 【105†Malhar Rao Holkar III】 succeeded Yashwantrao Holkar. His mother, Tulsabai Holkar, looked after the administration. However, with the help of 【106†Pathans】, 【107†Pindaris】, and the British, Dharama Kunwar and Balaram Seth plotted to imprison Tulsabai and Malharrao.[【90†citation needed】] When Tulsabai learnt about this, she beheaded both of them in 1815 and appointed Tantia Jog.
L176: As a result, Gaffur Khan Pindari secretly signed a treaty with the British on 9 November 1817 and killed Tulsabai on 19 December 1817.[【90†citation needed】]
L177: The treaty was signed on 6 January 1818 at 【108†Mandsaur】. Bhimabai Holkar did not accept the treaty, and kept attacking the British by guerilla methods. Years later, in revolt of 1857, 【109†Rani Lakshmibai】 of Jhansi took inspiration from Bhimabai Holkar and also fought against the British.
L178: At the conclusion of the 【110†Third Anglo-Maratha War】, the Holkars lost much of their territory to the British and were incorporated into the 【111†British Raj】 as a 【76†princely state】 of the 【112†Central India Agency】. The capital was shifted from Bhanpura to Indore.[【90†citation needed】]
L179: ## Princely state
L180: 
L181: Main article: 【70†Indore State】
L182: 
L183: 【328†Image†en.wikipedia.org】A silver rupee of Shivajirao Holkar 1886–1903, minted at Indore in 【113†Vikram Samvat】 1948 (1891)
L184: Malharrao Holkar III entered Indore on 2 November 1818. Tantia Jog was appointed his 【114†Diwan】 as he was a minor. As the old palace had been destroyed by the army of Daulat Rao Scindia, a new palace was constructed in its place. Malharrao III was succeeded by 【115†Marthand Rao Holkar】, who formally ascended to the throne on 17 January 1834. He was replaced by 【116†Hari Rao Holkar】, nephew of Yashwantrao, who ascended to the throne on 17 April 1834.
L185: He adopted 【117†Khande Rao Holkar II】 on 2 July 1841 and died on 24 October 1843. Khanderao was formally installed as the ruler on 13 November 1843, but he suddenly died on 17 February 1844. 【118†Tukojirao Holkar II】 (1835–1886) was installed on the throne on 27 June 1844. During the 【119†Indian Rebellion of 1857】, he was loyal to the 【120†British East India Company】. In October 1872, he appointed 【121†T. Madhava Rao】 as the Diwan of Indore.
L186: He died on 17 June 1886 and was succeeded by his eldest son, 【122†Shivajirao Holkar】.[【90†citation needed】]
L187: 【66†Yashwantrao Holkar II】 (reigned 1926–1948) ruled Indore state until shortly after India's independence in 1947, when he acceded to the Union of India. 【68†Indore】 became a district of 【123†Madhya Bharat】 state, which was merged into 【124†Madhya Pradesh】 state in 1956.[【90†citation needed】]
