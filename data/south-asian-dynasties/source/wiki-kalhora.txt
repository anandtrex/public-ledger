L134: The Kalhora dynasty (【51†Sindhi】: ڪلهوڙا خاندان, 【60†romanized】: Kalhōṛā khāndān) was a 【61†Sindhi Muslim】 dynasty primally based in the region of 【62†Sindh】 in present-day 【63†Pakistan】. From 1701 to 1768, their capital was 【49†Khudabad】 after which it was shifted to 【50†Hyderabad】.^{【64†[2]】} The northern half of 【65†Kutch district】 was also under the Kalhora Dynasty.^{【66†[3]】}
L135: ## History
L136: 
L137: [【67†edit】]
L138: 
L139: Kalhora rule of Sindh began around the start of the 18th century when 【55†Yar Muhammad Kalhoro】 was invested with title of Khuda Yar Khan and was made 【68†subahdar】 of upper Sindh by royal decree of the Mughals. Later, after his death, his son was additionally appointed Subahdar of 【69†Sehwan】 and thus oversaw most of Sindh.^{【70†[4]】}
L140: The Kalhora dynasty succumbed to the 【71†invasion】 of 【72†Nader Shah】. 【73†Sind】 was then conquered by 【74†Ahmad Shah Durrani】 in 1748–1750 making it a vassal of the 【75†Durrani Empire】.^{【76†[5]】}^{【77†[6]】}
L141: The Kalhora nawabs also participated in the 【78†Third Battle of Panipat】 as part of the victorious Islamic coalition. 【79†Ghulam Shah Kalhoro】 brought stability to Sindh by restructuring the state. His successors could not control Sindh for long and were overthrown by 【58†Talpur】 【80†mirs】 in the 【81†Battle of Halani】.
L142: Abdul Nabi Kalhoro was the last Kalhora ruler, being overthrown in 1783.^{【70†[4]】} He went on to govern Layyah and Bhakkar from 1787 to 1790 after defeating the 【82†Jaskani】 tribe.^{【83†[7]】}^{【84†[8]】}^{【85†[9]】}
L143: In 1793, the Afghan ruler 【86†Timur Shah】 died and was succeeded by Zaman Shah who demanded from Abdul Nabi arrears of one lakh and twenty thousand rupees. Abdul Nabi failed to pay the dues and did not comply with the orders of the Afghan court, and as a result his 【87†jagir】 was cancelled and granted to Nawab Muhammad Khan Saddozai.^{【83†[7]】}
L144: 
L145: According to 【88†Ḳāniʿ】, who visited their court, the Kalhora spoke 【51†Sindhi】 in court.^{【52†[1]】}
L146: ## Military
L147: 
L148: [【89†edit】]
L149: 
L150: The military of the Kalhora dynasty was mainly divided into two groups the Sariwal and the Sarfarosh. The Sariwal included the group that was better at ambushing the enemy camp while the Sarfarosh group comprised the best fighters who specialized in swordsmanship.^{【90†[10]】}
