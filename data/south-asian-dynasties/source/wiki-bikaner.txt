L161: Bikaner State was a 【85†princely state】 in the north-western most part of the 【66†Rajputana】 province of imperial 【86†British India】 from 1818 to 1947. The founder of the state, 【87†Rao Bika】, was a younger son of 【88†Rao Jodha】, the ruler and founder of the city of 【89†Jodhpur】 in 【90†Marwar】. Rao Bika chose to establish his own kingdom instead of inheriting his father's.
L162: Bika defeated the Jat clans of 【91†Jangladesh】, which today refers to the north and north-western 【79†Rajasthan】, along with his uncle Rao Kandhal and his adviser Vikramji Rajpurohit, whereupon he founded his own kingdom. Its capital was the city of 【92†Bikaner】. The emergence of the Mughal Empire witnessed the Kingdom of Bikaner pledging fealty to Mughal emperors, which led to the Kingdom becoming a loyal constituent of the Empire.
L163: Covering a vast area of 60,391 km^{2} (23,317 sq mi), Bikaner State was the second largest state under the 【93†Rajputana Agency】 after 【89†Jodhpur State】, with a revenue of Rs. 26,00,000 in 1901.^{【94†[2]】} Acquiescing to the 1947 call of 【95†Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel】 to integrate the princely states into the new independent India, Bikaner's then ruler, 【96†Lt General Maharaja Sir Sadul Singh】, advised by his 【97†dewan】 【98†K. M.
L164: Panikkar】, was one of the first rulers of a princely state to be receptive to integrating with the 【99†Indian Union】. The Maharaja of Bikaner set an example for the other heads of the princely states of India to follow by issuing a public appeal in April 1947 to join the 【100†Constituent Assembly of India】.^{【101†[3]】}
L165: The state was noted for the 【102†Bikaner style of miniature painting】.^{【103†[4]】}
L166: ## History
L167: 
L168: [【104†edit】]
L169: 
L170: Main article: 【66†History of Bikaner】
L171: The state of Bikaner was founded in 1465. During the reign of the Mughal Empire under Akbar's tutelage, Bikaner State became a loyal vassal of the Empire. It became a 【105†British protectorate】 on 9 March 1818. They were accorded a 17 【106†gun salute】 by the British authorities. Around the time of Indian independence and the 【107†partition of India】, the territory of the state of Bikaner came to share a border with 【108†Pakistan】.
L172: The 【109†accession】 to the Indian Union was signed by the Maharaja on 7 August 1947.^{【110†[5]】}
L173: ## Rulers
L174: 
L175: [【111†edit】]
L176: 
L177: The rulers belong to the 【112†Rathore】 【113†Rajputs】 of the 【114†Suryavanshi】 lineage or of Solar descent representing a third junior branch of the parent ruling family of 【115†Jodhpur】,^{【116†[6]】} the second being 【117†Idar】 yet Bikaner was placed second on the basis of area, wealth and power by the colonial 【86†British India】.

L329: ## Orders of chivalry
L330: 
L331: [【152†edit】]
L332: The Royal House of Bikaner awarded two dynastic orders, the Order of the Star of Honour and the Order of Vikram Star.
L333: 【83†Maharaja Ganga Singh】 established the Order of the Star of Honour in six grades in order to "mark his golden jubilee on the throne".^{【153†[8]】} The 【154†Order of the Vikram Star†nl.wikipedia.org】 was established in 1944 by 【96†Maharaja Sadul Singh of Bikaner】 in five grades "to recognize services to the state." The first grade (Grand Commander) includes a cordon with jewel, along with a breast star.^{【153†[8]】} The breast star features "Twelve alternate petals of gold and silver overlapping and radiating from a central motif showing the 【155†Goddess Karni】 blessing Rao Bikaji who is standing with lance in hand next to his horse."^{【156†[9]】} A red enamelled diamond in the center of the breast star, which is surrounded by a wreath, contains the inscription in the 【157†Devanagari】 script Shri Karni Aasisadi Bikatothirraj (Blessing by Karni Mati for his Perpetual Rule).^{【156†[9]】}
