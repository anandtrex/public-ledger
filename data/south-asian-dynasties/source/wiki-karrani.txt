L136: The Karrani dynasty (【62†Pashto】: کرلاڼي, 【63†romanized】: Karlāṇī, 【64†Bengali】: কররাণী, 【65†romanized】: Kôrrāṇī) was a late-medieval royal dynasty that ruled the restored 【54†Bengal Sultanate】 between 1564 and 1576. Founded by 【56†Taj Khan Karrani】, an 【66†Afghan】 from the 【67†Pashtun】 【68†Karlani】 tribe hailing from 【53†Bangash】,^{【69†[1]】} it was the last independent dynasty to rule 【70†Bengal】, before the region became a 【71†Subah】 of the 【72†Mughal Empire】.
L137: ## History
L138: 
L139: [【73†edit】]
L140: ### Founding
L141: 
L142: [【74†edit】]
L143: Taj Khan was formerly an employee of the Sur Emperor 【75†Sher Shah Suri】. From 1562 to 1564, Taj Khan captured south-eastern 【76†Bihar】 and 【77†West Bengal】, and with his assassination of the last Muhammed Shahi ruler, he seized all of 【70†Bengal】. The capital was at 【78†Gaur】. Taj Khan was followed by his brother 【58†Sulaiman Khan Karrani】, who shifted the seat of government from Gaur to 【61†Tanda】 (also in 【79†Malda】) in 1565.
L144: In 1568, Sulaiman Khan 【80†annexed Orissa to the Karrani sultanate】 permanently. Nominally he accepted sovereignty of the 【72†Mughal】 Emperor 【81†Akbar】, and his prime minister Lodi Khan placated the Mughals with gifts and banqueting.^{【82†[2]】} Sulaiman Khan's authority extended from 【83†Koch Bihar】 to 【84†Puri】, and from 【85†Son River】 to 【86†Brahmaputra River】.^{【87†[3]】}
L145: ### Mughal invasion
L146: 
L147: [【88†edit】]
L148: On 25 September 1574, the 【72†Mughal】 general 【89†Munim Khan】 captured the Karrani capital 【61†Tanda】. The 【90†Battle of Tukaroi】 fought on 3 March 1575 forced 【57†Daud Khan Karrani】, the last Karrani ruler, to withdraw to Orissa. The battle led to the Treaty of Katak in which Daud ceded the whole of Bengal and Bihar, retaining only Orissa. The treaty eventually failed after the death of Munim Khan at the age of 50 in October 1575.
L149: Daud Khan took the opportunity and invaded Bengal, declaring independence from Akbar. The Mughal onslaught against the Karrani sultanate ended with the 【91†Battle of Rajmahal】 on 12 July 1576, led by the Mughal general 【92†Khan Jahan I】. Daud Khan was executed. However, the Pashtuns and the local landlords known as 【93†Baro Bhuyans】 led by 【94†Isa Khan】 continued to resist the Mughal invasion.
L150: Later in 1612 during the reign of 【95†Jahangir】, Bengal was decisively consolidated as a Mughal province.^{【96†[4]】}
