L125: Khadga dynasty (Bengali: 【50†[kʰɔɽɡo rad͡ʒbɔŋʃo]】), was a 【51†Buddhist】 dynasty which ruled the areas of 【52†Vanga】 and 【53†Samatata】 in ancient Bengal from 625 CE to 716 CE.^{【54†[2]】} Chronologically, the dynasty emerged as a powerful kingdom of Bengal between the fall of 【44†Gauda Kingdom】 and the rise of the 【47†Pala Empire】.
L126: Their ascendancy may have been immediately preceded by the overthrow of a previously ruling 【45†Bhadra dynasty】.^{【55†[3]】} While they did not assume imperial titles, the Khadgas retained sovereignty over the ancient kingdom of Vanga and later conquered Samatata.^{【56†[4]】} It was succeeded by the 8th-9th century Buddhist 【57†Deva dynasty】.^{【58†[5]】}
